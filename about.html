About

Tamara Baird is the founder of Baird Forge Inc, a technology development practice focused on building human-centered tools and learning experiences that make complex systems safe, understandable, and usable in high-stakes environments. Her work bridges simulation and educational design with emerging technology — turning difficult systems into practical tools and knowledge that people can rely on.

With over a decade of experience in nursing education, simulation design for both healthcare and cybersecurity domains, Tamara brings an evidence-based perspective to every project she builds. At Baird Forge, she creates experiments, prototypes, and tools at the intersection of technical education and human-centered technology. Work is documented and iterated on openly where possible, with select projects maturing into full releases, learning resources, or commercial products.
